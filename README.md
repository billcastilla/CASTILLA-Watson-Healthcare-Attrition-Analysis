# Watson Healthcare Attrition Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns in a synthetic healthcare dataset using Python and pandas. The goal is to identify factors associated with employee departures and provide data-driven recommendations to support employee retention.

## Business Problem

Employee turnover can increase recruitment costs, disrupt operations, and affect workforce stability. This analysis explores which employee characteristics and workplace conditions are associated with higher attrition.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python

- Pandas

- Statistical analysis

- Jupyter Notebook

- GitHub

## Key Findings

- **Overall Attrition:** 11.87% of employees left.

- **Overtime:** Employees working overtime had a 29.20% attrition rate, compared with 5.00% for employees not working overtime.

- **Work-life balance:** Employees with a Work-Life Balance rating of 1 had a 26.67% attrition rate.

- **Job level:** Level 1 employees had a 22.87% attrition rate.

- **Monthly income:** Employees who left had a lower average monthly income than employees who stayed.

## Recommendations

- Review overtime demands and workload distribution.

- Prioritize retention efforts for Level 1 employees.

- Investigate the combined effects of overtime and lower Work-Life Balance ratings.

- Evaluate compensation patterns alongside other factors associated with attrition.
